## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Yüzyılın İzinde ("In the Footsteps of the Century") is a silhouette-style endless runner that tells the hundred-year story of the Republic of Türkiye through scenes flowing in the background.

Your journey starts with the opening of the Grand National Assembly in 1920 and runs all the way to 2026. Your character first walks, then picks up the pace and starts to run. While you leap over obstacles, history unfolds behind you: the proclamation of the Republic, the alphabet reform, the opening of the Bosphorus Bridge, the Republic's centennial and much more.

★ 105 key events, 67 hand-made scenes
Political, social and economic milestones and natural disasters, each shown in a scene that fits its year and place, described in a neutral, encyclopedic tone. Every event comes with a few obstacle-free seconds so you can read it comfortably. (Event texts are in Turkish.)

★ A world that changes with time
Ankara, Istanbul, rural Anatolia, coastal towns and industrial cities. As the years pass, houses, mosques, vehicles, clothing and skylines change, and the film look moves from sepia to black-and-white to color.

★ Simple to play, hard to master
Tap to jump, hold for a higher and longer jump, tap again in the air to double jump. Collect lights: every 50 lights bring back a lost life.

★ Moments of remembrance
During earthquakes and days of mourning, your character slows down and bows their head.

★ Leaderboard
Compare your longest journey with players everywhere through Google Play Games.

★ Free, no ads
No ads, no in-app purchases, no personal data collection. Playable offline.
```
